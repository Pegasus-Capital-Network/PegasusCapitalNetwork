Need a decision-ready AI acquisition target brief?

Our pilot research service screens five to eight publicly identifiable AI and B2B SaaS businesses against your stated acquisition criteria. The written brief separates documented information from seller claims, flags risks such as weak retention, platform dependence and non-transferable software, and explains why each target does or does not fit. It is independent commercial research — not seller representation, brokerage, negotiation or a promise of an introduction.
