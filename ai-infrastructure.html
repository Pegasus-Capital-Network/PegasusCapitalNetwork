Market Intelligence

Public market signals

Transactions, site announcements, power deals, buyer activity and new technology reported in public sources, with a link to the original source.

  • Who is involved, where, and the price or megawatts if disclosed
  • Always linked to the first-hand source
  • Not an offer, and not a claim that anyone will transact
Marketplace

Verified offers and requests

Assets and mandates submitted directly by the owner, buyer or their authorized representative through a Pegasus profile, and reviewed before they appear.

  • Submitted by the principal or with written authority
  • Key facts checked: control of the asset, location, stated power
  • Shared on a no-names basis until both sides agree to connect

A two-sided market.

Owners can offer an asset. Buyers can post exactly what they want to acquire. Both work through your existing Pegasus profile; there is no separate platform to join.

01 / OFFER

I have an asset

A data center, powered land, a power project, equipment, or a project that needs capital.

  • Type of asset and location (city or county is enough to start)
  • Available or planned power, in MW, and its status
  • What you are looking for: sale, JV, financing, offtake

02 / ACQUIRE

A buyer, operator, AI company or investor with a specific requirement for sites, capacity or projects.
