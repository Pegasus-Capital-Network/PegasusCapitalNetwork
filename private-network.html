Investment risks & important disclosures
Is this an investment offer?
No. This page is an informational resource to establish professional relationships. It is not an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy any security or investment. Any offer would be made only through applicable disclosure documents to eligible investors.
Does Pegasus guarantee returns or deals?
No. Pegasus does not guarantee that a suitable opportunity exists, that financing will be arranged, or that any transaction will close. Real estate and private investments are speculative, illiquid and involve substantial risk, including the possible loss of capital.
Who can participate?
We focus on qualified investment organizations. Eligibility, accredited-investor status and KYC/AML are confirmed later through a separate, secure process, and specific opportunities are subject to applicable securities laws, including for non-U.S. investors.
What about tokenization?
Digital-securities infrastructure is an area we are studying, not a live product. Tokens are subject to securities laws, transfer restrictions and custody arrangements; a token does not automatically grant ownership of real property or guarantee liquidity.
