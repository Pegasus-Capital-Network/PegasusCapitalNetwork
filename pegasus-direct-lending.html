What secures your investment
Recorded deed of trust
You hold a direct, recorded, foreclosable claim on real property, in your own name.
Conservative loan-to-value
We lend against a disciplined share of value, so there is equity beneath your position.
Title & hazard insurance
A lender's title policy and evidence of property insurance are part of every closing.
Independent escrow & servicing
Neutral escrow handles closing; a servicer collects and remits your payments and tracks payoff.
Borrower has skin in the game
Business-purpose loans where the borrower holds real equity in the property.
You own the note — no pooling
You invest in a specific loan you chose, not a blind share of a fund.
